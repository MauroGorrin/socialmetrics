## Executive Summary

The biggest weakness is not the messaging — it is the **complete absence of growth infrastructure**. The site is a brochure that captures only decision-ready traffic. The site is structurally incapable of capturing organic search demand regardless of how good the content is.

Several smaller but high-leverage issues compound the problem: a broken urgency banner (missing number in template variable), a Gmail contact address on a site charging €3,000–5,000+/month, and testimonials with no company attribution that sophisticated buyers will discount.
